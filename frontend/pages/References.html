PROMPT LOG
+
Wireframing and hi-fi design prompts used to build this project, kept as a plain-text log for transparency.
Design Session: ArtConnect Homepage and Gallery (High Fidelity) Scope: Dark, gallery at night high fidelity design for the Homepage and Gallery screens, full bleed scrolling from top to bottom. Direction established: Moody aesthetic inspired by Diverso, paired with Cormorant, Archivo, and Space Mono typography, with a deep petrol accent color. Issue resolved: A persistent sub pixel seam appeared between the hero section and the Featured Works section. Fix: the hero spotlight was separated into an absolute positioned layer that fades to transparent above the section boundary, leaving the lower hero and Featured Works as one continuous flat surface (#0d0e10) with no rendered edge. Controls added (via CSS variables): • Accent palette: toggle between petrol, bronze, plum, and sage themes. • Gallery lighting: a night to day dimmer affecting spotlight and frame glow together. • Frame matting: toggle between matted and full bleed artwork cards. Layout refinements: Hero scrim direction changed from left to right to top to bottom behind the featured painting; the prior left scrim was removed; a subtle vertical gradient was added to the Gallery grid background. Status: All changes merged and live. Next steps under consideration: Extend high fidelity design to the remaining five screens (Events, Event Detail Modal, Submit Artwork, FAQ, Reference) in the same dark gallery mood, or continue iterating on Homepage and Gallery.

FAQ

How do I submit my artwork? +
Fill out the Submit form with your details, category, and images. Our curators review every piece within two weeks.
Is there a submission fee? +
No. Submission and first-sale listing are free. We only ask that you keep your profile and pricing up to date.
Can I sell my work through ArtConnect? +
Yes. Mark a piece for sale with a price, or list it as "Not for sale" if it's for display only.
Do you take a commission? +
We take a modest 10% commission on sales made through the gallery, waived entirely on an artist's first sale.
How do I attend an event? +
RSVP from the Events page — most evenings are free, some ticketed events note a price at checkout.
Can I visit without an appointment? +
Yes, we're open to walk-ins Tuesday–Sunday, 11:00–18:00. Group visits of six or more should book ahead.
